# Scheduled tasks

Say "In 30 minutes, say hello" and the agent schedules a one-time callback right away, in the same conversation tab unless you ask for a separate thread. Recurring schedules, edits, and deletions ask you to confirm first. The scheduling card's **View scheduled task** shows the saved time and destination.

A callback waits while its task is busy. Removing its tab, or archiving or deleting its task, pauses its unfinished schedules, and restoring the task leaves them paused. To resume one, choose an available conversation, and a new time if the original one-off time has passed.

Schedules run on time only while Alveary is running and the Mac is awake. Runs missed in the past seven days catch up after launch or wake.
